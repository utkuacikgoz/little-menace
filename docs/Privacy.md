# Little Menace privacy

Updated 25 September 2026.

Little Menace stores your gremlin's name, needs, progress, outfits, preferences and recent dialogue on your device. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking SDK or analytics service. We do not receive your gameplay data.

Your saved game is kept in the app's local storage and may be included in your normal device backup. Start Over in Settings resets the current pet and its progress. Removing the app removes its local storage; device backups are managed through your device settings.

Optional reminders are scheduled on your device. You can disable them in the app or in iOS Settings. Share cards leave the app only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Optional purchases are handled by Apple. The app reads verified purchase entitlements to unlock your collection; we do not receive your payment details. Resetting your pet does not remove your purchases. Restore Purchases is available in Settings and on the collection page.

For questions, use the [support page](Support.md). If you submit a GitHub issue, its contents are public and GitHub processes that submission under its own privacy policy.
