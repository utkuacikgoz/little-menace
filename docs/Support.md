# Little Menace support

[Report a problem or send feedback](https://github.com/utkuacikgoz/little-menace/issues/new).

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, what you tried and what happened. GitHub issues are public, so leave out payment details, receipts and personal information.

**A collection is missing:** use Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Midnight Snack page, with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

**The store is unavailable:** previews and ordinary play still work. Use Try store again when you have a connection. A purchase requires the product to be available through Apple in your storefront.

**The gremlin refuses a snack or nap:** a full gremlin saves snacks for later; a rested one wants to play. Naps end automatically when energy is full or after 25 minutes.

**Reminders:** turn them off in Settings, or manage Little Menace's notification permission in iOS Settings.
